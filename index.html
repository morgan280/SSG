The Store · Stop · Go system

Three lights. One proven road
from raw dirt to open doors.

Site selection is where retail is won or lost. Our three-phase system takes every location through the same discipline — so the site you build on is the site the numbers already chose.

Store

We find the site

Every engagement starts on the road. We drive the corridors, run the counts, and find the corners — often before they ever hit the market.

  • 24/7 traffic counts (AADT) on every candidate
  • Drive-time demographics & growth models
  • Visibility, access & egress scoring
  • Off-market parcel sourcing
Stop

We secure the deal

When a site scores, we move. We negotiate, vet, and buy the ground ourselves — so your brand never has to chase a listing or a landowner.

  • Purchase, option & assignment structures
  • Title, survey & Phase I due diligence
  • Zoning, entitlements & curb cuts
  • Utility & access agreements
Go

You open the doors

We deliver construction-ready sites with the paperwork done and the path cleared — then stay on through build-out until the lights come on.

  • Construction-ready handoff, title clean
  • Permit-ready site packages
  • Owner's-rep coordination through build-out
  • Post-open performance reviews
